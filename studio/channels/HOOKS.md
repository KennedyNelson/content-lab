# Hook rule (owner, 2026-11-21): applies to FunWithKen YouTube and the new educational Instagram account (always). kaalujokes Instagram: OPTIONAL, see the exception below.

**Exception, kaalujokes (owner correction 2026-11-21):** NOT a hard rule. Real-life relatable scenario reels (Kalu and his girlfriend, Kalu and Battery, call/phone chats, everyday situations) must NOT open with a hook line, because a hook gives away the ending and ruins the punchline. They start straight inside the situation. Hook-led reels (what-if, shocking claim) are still allowed on kaalujokes. Mix both, decided by the script: if announcing the joke up front would spoil the payoff, use no hook.

The starting hook is the most important part of every video. It decides whether the viewer stays to the end. Write 5 candidate hooks per video and keep the strongest.
- First line, first second: spoken and/or shown. It must make the viewer stop and wait for the payoff.
- Hook shapes that work: a "what if" ("What if the Moon disappeared tonight?"); a high-stakes claim ("This is the most asked coding question at Google"); an instantly relatable moment ("When your code works and you don't know why"); a crazy specific true fact; a puzzle or question the viewer wants answered.
- No greetings, no intros, no "today we will learn". The hook is the first words, not a title card.
- Keep a curiosity gap open until near the end, then deliver the answer clearly.
- For knowledge hooks the claim must be true; fact-check and keep the source in the script file.
