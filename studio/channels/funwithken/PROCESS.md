# FunWithKen daily process (copied from the old skill 2026-11-21; funwithken/RULES.md and STYLE-SHEET.md win; ignore any "last two looks" rule; use the repo KennedyNelson/content-lab)
## Daily process, in order
1. **Research.** Do the pacing research of the PACE AND ENGAGEMENT section (Composio scrapecreators for YouTube when it has credits, otherwise web search). Also search the web, especially YouTube Shorts and what is trending and shared right now (AI and Claude humour, science, space, the human body, history, survival, 'what if' questions, unnoticed everyday things, relatable boy/girl/couple behaviour). See which kinds of videos in the niche get huge views, take the good bits, then create our OWN idea. First pick today's CONTENT MIX type. Then apply the TOPIC SPICE TEST: pick the single topic with the strongest viral potential, relatability and a jaw-dropping extreme fact (knowledge) or an instantly relatable, darkly funny or moving idea (other types). Clone the repo and read `studio/PUBLISHED.md`: never repeat an idea or script. The same FORMAT may return (another chat short) but with a completely new scenario, jokes and look.
2. **Choose the look.** If a person is needed, it is Kalu in Desi Goggle-Eye Toon (see CHARACTERS); otherwise pick from the STYLE PICKER in `shorts-animation-styles` (match the idea, ). For built-in faceless-shorts styles load the `faceless-shorts` skill. Skip any intake or 'Build it?' steps: use defaults.
3. **Script.** Length and word count come from the pacing brief (past videos for reference only: explainers about 55-75 words in 25-35 s; chat and banter 7-10 short lines in 15-25 s). Extreme hook first (a jaw-dropping true fact for knowledge videos; a relatable setup line or a striking image for the other types), ONE main idea, a funny twist, a last line that loops back to the start, funniest beat early. Original wording only. Write the PACING BRIEF first, then run the NEVER BORING and HUMOUR checklists and rewrite until they pass. Fact-check against reliable sources and list them in `script.json`. Title: short, curious, under 60 characters, built around the extreme fact or person where possible. Fix every line, tone and emotion tag now.
4. **Vary the setting** every video: set, props, side characters and their outfits, music. Kalu himself never changes. Log it.
5. **Voiceover** with ElevenLabs model `eleven_v4` (Starter plan, 30,234 credits a month) using the toolkit in `shorts-animation-styles`: check the live balance first, write each line like a director, retake flat lines. The YouTube description needs 'Voices: ElevenLabs'.
6. **Build** the video with the chosen style (its stylesheet and the AUDIO-DRIVEN BUILD in `shorts-animation-styles`). Check frames at low quality, build the sound mix, render at full quality.
7. **QA (required).** Render a contact sheet and LOOK at the frames. Check which CONTENT MIX type it is and that it delivers it (knowledge: the viewer can say in one sentence what they learned and every fact is true; relatable: instantly recognisable and sendable; dark: funny and inside the limits; emotional: the feeling lands), and check length, captions match audio, lip-sync is on the right speaker, no glitches, audio not silent, nothing offensive, facts correct, and that it matches its pacing brief and passes the PACE AND ENGAGEMENT principles and the NEVER BORING list (never static, easy to follow, funny, no on-screen counters or labels, hook is the most extreme true fact, engaging to the last frame). Fix and re-render. If it still fails, upload PRIVATE and report; never publish public.
8. **Host.** Re-encode (`ffmpeg -nostdin -i out.mp4 -c:v libx264 -crf 17 -preset slow -maxrate 12M -bufsize 24M -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -movflags +faststart YYYY-MM-DD-slug.mp4`), copy to the repo root, commit and push to main. (Google Drive cannot be used from the sandbox, so GitHub raw links replace the Drive step.) Delete older videos from the repo after they are posted.
9. **Publish.** Zapier YouTube `upload_video` with video = `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KennedyNelson/content-lab/main/<file>.mp4`: privacy public, the title unchanged, description of one or two catchy sentences plus 3-5 hashtags (include #Shorts, and 'Voices: ElevenLabs' whenever ElevenLabs voices are used), 5 tags, the best category (knowledge videos: 28 Science & Technology or 27 Education; relatable, dark and emotional videos: 23 Comedy or 24 Entertainment; never the default), made_for_kids false, language en. More than one video a day is fine: if something was already posted today, still publish public. Every run publishes public, scheduled or manual; the schedule fires once a day and any extra run started by hand also goes public. PRIVATE is only for a video that fails QA. Permanently deleting a video is not allowed for Claude: set it private and tell the owner to delete it in YouTube Studio.
10. **Log.** Append to `studio/PUBLISHED.md` (date, channel YouTube, content type, idea, style, title, link, look, voices, credits used, pace choices; and chat looks in `studio/LOOKLOG.md`) and push.
11. **Report** to the owner: title, link, ElevenLabs credits used and left, anything fixed during QA. On failure say which step and why.
