# FunWithKen style sheet (live again 2026-11-21; trial of a few days)
Carried over from owner feedback so far:
- Clean and real: no exaggeration, no unnecessary objects or repeated dialogue, understated acting, story visually clear.
- Natural voice speed (never slow), tight pauses, voices carry the mood through tone.
- Hook in the first second with the most extreme true fact or image; no intros.
- Captions only on screen (setup line allowed for relatable reel styles).
- Advertiser-friendly: no hate, no sexual content, no slurs; the edge sits in the situation.
- Four video types mixed (funny knowledge, relatable comedy, dark/spicy within limits, emotional); characters not required, faceless informational styles allowed; Kalu leads when a person is needed.
- Style chosen per idea by Claude from the whole library, repeats allowed.
Defaults while the owner has not decided: mix of funny knowledge, relatable comedy, mild dark comedy and emotional; informational, modern, trendy and relatable; one video a day, 9 am IST.

## Hook (owner rule 2026-11-21, all channels)
Every video needs a strong opening hook in the first second. See ../HOOKS.md. Teaching tone: explain as if to a 5-year-old, quick and engaging.
