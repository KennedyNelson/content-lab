# Sports community style sheet (provisional, 2026-11-21: nothing approved yet)
## Feel
Energetic, welcoming, team-first. Bold, bright and fast: dark navy with a neon lime accent, big wide headlines, action photos, numbers and dates huge. Reads from far away on a flyer or banner and in one second on a phone.

## Colour and type (brand.css; owner to decide)
Navy #0a1a2f, white text, panels #13294a, accent lime #c6ff00 (text on accent navy). Display: Archivo Black (uppercase). Body: Montserrat.

## Layout
- Date, place and time are the second-biggest thing after the headline. One call to action (join, register, scan the QR). Action photo cropped tight, with a fade into navy.
- Flyer: headline + photo top, "when / where / who / how to join" block, QR + contact bottom. Pamphlet: why join, schedule and venues, rules/fees, how to register, sponsor strip if provided.

## Video and animation
- Reels: fast cuts on the beat, slow-motion hero moments, kinetic captions, counters (members, matches, goals), scoreboard and bracket animations, confetti/particle burst on wins, whistle/crowd SFX from studio/sfx_library.
- Recruitment reel: hook ("Looking for players?"), what you get, how to join, date. Event promo: countdown, venue, highlight shots, ends on QR/link.

## Message and hooks (English)
Core idea: "No team? No problem. Just show up." Free, open to everyone, any level. Hook examples: "Want to play but have nobody to play with?" / "Cricket, football, badminton, running. Free. Every week." / "Beginner? Perfect." / "Bring nothing but interest." Sport pictograms (bat, ball, shuttle, running shoe) as simple icons in the accent colour; one sport per piece plus an "all sports" version.
