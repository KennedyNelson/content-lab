# Instagram style sheet (draft from approved reels and owner notes, Nov 2026; owner to confirm)
Benchmark reels: date-poop Stickman reel (approved, "dialogue speed, changing frames and camera angles were perfect"); Kalu interrupted-by-a-call reel (posted 2026-11-20).

## Look
- Style is chosen per idea by Claude from the whole library (Stickman, Comic Art, Pixel, Remotion, Lemo styles). No rule against repeating a style.
- Fast cuts: new camera angle or zoom punch every 1 to 2.5 s, close-ups on reactions, a visual last beat. Never one locked angle for a whole reel.
- Characters: Kalu is the lead. Outfit, hairstyle and voice change per reel; Battery's top knot and Priya's ponytail are fixed.
- Setup line at the top in the first frame (reel's own language). No other unnecessary text on screen: no sound words, no wall text, no labels. Word captions at the bottom are fine.

## Sound
- Dialogue natural and fast, tight gaps, no dead air. A punchline is one continuous take (no pauses between words); questions must sound like questions.
- Voices: normal pitch, emotion through tone (slightly angry means slightly). Never deep or girlish unless asked. ElevenLabs eleven_v4, each line generated separately.
- SFX only from the owner's library. Do not end two reels in a row with the same sound. Video ends right on the SFX; hold one reaction until then.
- Check every line for a repeated word (silence-burst count) and send the voice lines to the owner for review; Claude cannot hear audio.

## Humour and language
- Hindi/Hinglish follows the owner's wording exactly. Dark, crude, relatable humour is wanted, inside Claude's limits (no hate or group-targeting, no explicit sex).
- Owner supplies the idea and jokes; Claude does not invent them.

## Posting
- Never post before the owner says so. Caption 2 to 5 words plus exactly 5 hashtags. Log in PUBLISHED.md; read insights after 24 to 48 h.
- Length decided by the joke, usually 12 to 20 s.
