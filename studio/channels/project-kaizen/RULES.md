# Project Kaizen rules (current only)
- Replies to the owner in English only. Deliver files with SendUserFile; the owner posts. No posting account is connected; never post without a set-up and an OK.
- Language: English for all copy, captions and voice.
- Core theme, always apply: continuous improvement, "1% better every day"; the merch spreads that motivation. Fonts are not fixed: choose per piece to match this theme. The Yujiro shirt is named "THE OGRE - YUJIRO HANMA T-Shirt" in all copy.
- Brand: look comes from brand.css (colours from the owner's techpacks: black and white only; blue only for the Arise product; it exists but is not on the site, ask before promoting). Logo only from studio/channels/project-kaizen/assets (owner's files); never redraw or alter the antlers.
- Product truth: real product photos only. No AI-made images of the actual garments. AI may make backgrounds, textures or mood art around a real photo.
- Copy truth: prices, discounts, sizes, delivery claims, dates and codes only from the owner or projectkaizen.in at the time. No health, weight-loss or performance claims. No fake scarcity (countdown or "only 3 left") unless it is real.
- Third-party characters and artwork: THE OGRE - YUJIRO HANMA t-shirt (Baki), the Toji "Obsession" design and any other design based on a third-party character (his folder also has files named baki and toji): show only the owner's real photos of the product exactly as it is. Never redraw, animate, recolour or generate that character; do not use the character in banners, flyers or videos beyond the real product photo. If the owner wants more, ask him to confirm he holds the licence.
- Music: only licensed/CC0 or the owner's own. SFX from studio/sfx_library.
- Video: 9:16 1080x1920 30 fps for reels and stories (nothing in the top 240 / bottom 350 px except background), 16:9 1920x1080 for web and YouTube, H.264, AAC 192k, -14 LUFS, draft first. Strong hook in the first second (../HOOKS.md). Hindi captions in Devanagari when the video is in Hindi.
- Images: sizes and print rules in ../../design/README.md. Always preview and read the text of every piece before sending.
- Effects pack: for any on-camera clip use ../../EFFECTS-GUIDE.md; one effect every 3 to 6 seconds, never over the face.
- Log nothing in PUBLISHED.md unless a piece is actually posted (name the account).
- Repo hygiene: the Yujiro character illustration (Yujiro.png and the Embrace Pain print) stays on the owner's Mac and out of the repo. Product facts (PRODUCTS.md) and the brand's own logos, tags and print lockups (assets/) are committed at reduced size. Originals: ~/Documents/ProjectKaizen and the techpack zips in ~/Downloads.
- On-screen text rule (owner 2026-11-21): never repeat text that is already printed on a shirt (DISCIPLINE, ARISE, EXECUTE REGARDLESS, IF NOT NOW WHEN, STAY HARD, EMBRACE PAIN, PROJECT KAIZEN, 1% BETTER EVERY DAY). Write fresh promotional lines instead, keep the frame clean, and use motion graphics and strong video effects. The theme (continuous improvement) is carried by new wording such as small steps, show up again, keep improving.
- Promo scope (owner 2026-11-21): all three tees are promoted together. Hang tag print will use @projectkaizenclo.
- Promo copy rule (owner 2026-11-21, after v1): no generic motivational lines on screen; focus on the products, video effects, motion graphics and animation. Allowed text: brand theme lines ("1% BETTER EVERY DAY", "KAIZEN MENTALITY"), true product facts (cut, fabric, sizes, made in India), prices from the site, offer, call to action, handle and site. For THE OGRE always show the back view (that is where the character is) when naming it; never say "meet the Ogre" over a front view.
- Standard ending for all promos (owner 2026-11-21): build the lockup from assets/logo-antlers-wordmark-tagline-white.png frame by frame: tagline letters one by one, then PROJECT KAIZEN letter by letter, then left antler, then right antler, then SHOP NOW, site and handle. No opening frame of text: start on product.
- Ad format (owner 2026-11-21): one separate 9:16 ad per tee (Discipline, Arise, The Ogre), each usable as an Instagram post and a Meta ad, plus one brand video (logo build). Product ads give the product the most screen time, show feature callouts (95/5 blend, DTF prints, 210 GSM, comfort for sports/gym/casual/streetwear/every occasion), then price, then a SHORT end card: punchline and PROJECT KAIZEN already in place, only the antlers animate (left, then right), then SHOP NOW, site, handle. The full letter-by-letter logo build lives only in the brand video.
