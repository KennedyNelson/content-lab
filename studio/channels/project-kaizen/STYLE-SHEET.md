# Project Kaizen style sheet (provisional, 2026-11-21: built from the brand's words, not yet from owner-approved work)
## Feel
Raw, disciplined, gym-and-street. Dark and high contrast with one hot accent. Big condensed uppercase headlines, short lines, lots of black. Motivational but never preachy. Looks like gear for people who train, also wearable on the street.

## Colour and type (brand.css; owner to confirm)
- Background near-black #0b0b0b, text bone white #f4f1ea, panels #1a1a1a, accent red-orange #ff3b1f (text on accent: black).
- Display: Anton (condensed, uppercase). Body: Montserrat 600-800. One accent word per headline in the accent colour.

## Layout
- One headline of 2 to 4 words, one supporting line, one call to action pill. Product photo big, cropped tight, bottom or side, fading into black. Handle bottom or top corner.
- Print: more air; headline 21 mm tall, details 4.6 mm, 8 mm inside trim.

## Video
- Fast: a new frame every 1 to 2 s. Hard cuts on the beat, zoom punches, kinetic caps type slamming in, slow-motion on the product movement, grain and a contrast grade. Sparks/dust (tsParticles) as accents, not constant. Hook in the first second ("1% better. Today.", "Discipline is a choice.").
- Ends on the product + wordmark + website. SFX: hits and whooshes sparingly from studio/sfx_library.
- Founder on camera (owner): cinematic grade, bold kinetic captions, effects from the pack.
