# Project Kaizen style sheet (2026-11-21: colours, logo and wording now taken from the owner's techpacks; layouts still provisional until he approves a piece)
## Feel
Raw, disciplined, gym-and-street. Dark and high contrast with one hot accent. Big condensed uppercase headlines, short lines, lots of black. Motivational but never preachy. Looks like gear for people who train, also wearable on the street.

## Colour and type (brand.css, from the techpacks)
- Monochrome: Jet Black (Pantone 19-0303 TCX, screen #111111) with Bright White (Pantone 11-0601 TCX, screen #f4f5f0) for print, rib and labels. Arise also exists in blue (owner's real photos 2026-11-21): blue only for that product. No red or other accent colour.
- Emphasis without a second colour: the accent word is a hollow white outline; CTA pill is white with black text.
- Display: Anton (close to the brand's condensed caps; Arise uses a distressed condensed font and the antler mark uses wide-tracked "1 % B E T T E R  E V E R Y  D A Y"). Body: Montserrat. Tagline always wide-tracked caps.
- Logo: white antler mark, studio/channels/project-kaizen/assets/logo-antlers-white.png (templates add it automatically). Wordmark "PROJECT KAIZEN" is a condensed bold caps text lockup: ask the owner for the vector wordmark file for large use.
- Signature elements from the garments: antlers, "1% BETTER EVERY DAY", flame side prints on Discipline, distressed type on Arise, QR "scan to check us out" on tags.

## Design language of the finished garments (analysed from the final art, 2026-11-21)
- Always white graphics on black. Nothing else: no gradients, no colour, no photos on the garment.
- Two kinds of lettering: (1) clean heavy condensed slab caps for the wordmark "PROJECT KAIZEN"; (2) the same slab with a worn, distressed (grunge, cracked) texture for the statements ARISE, DISCIPLINE, STAY HARD, EMBRACE PAIN, usually set big and tight, sometimes curved.
- Tagline "1 % B E T T E R  E V E R Y  D A Y" always in very wide-tracked small caps, under the antlers or wordmark.
- The antler mark is the symbol: symmetrical, sharp, thin-to-thick strokes, centred above type. Flames (Discipline sides) and a hard-line "stay hard" rule are secondary motifs.
- Statement + one supporting line: "DISCIPLINE / EXECUTE, REGARDLESS."; "ARISE / IF NOT NOW, WHEN?"; "STAY HARD"; "EMBRACE PAIN". Short, imperative, no exclamation marks.
- Tags and bag: centred antlers over the wordmark and tagline on black; back "THANK YOU ! FOR CHOOSING US" + QR + handle + site.
- For promo graphics, reuse this: white on near-black, slab caps (brand.css), distressed texture for the big statement word, antler logo as sign-off.

## Layout
- One headline of 2 to 4 words, one supporting line, one call to action pill. Product photo big, cropped tight, bottom or side, fading into black. Handle bottom or top corner.
- Print: more air; headline 21 mm tall, details 4.6 mm, 8 mm inside trim.

## Video
- Fast: a new frame every 1 to 2 s. Hard cuts on the beat, zoom punches, kinetic caps type slamming in, slow-motion on the product movement, grain and a contrast grade. Sparks/dust (tsParticles) as accents, not constant. Hook in the first second ("1% better. Today.", "Execute, regardless.", "If not now, when?").
- Ends on the antler logo + wordmark + projectkaizen.in. SFX: hits and whooshes sparingly from studio/sfx_library.
- Founder on camera (owner): cinematic grade, bold kinetic captions, effects from the pack.

## Image ads, human-made style (2026-11-22)
Owner feedback: the glitchy video ads read as "AI slop"; wanted natural, minimal, clean, aesthetic, human-made. Three still styles built from references, using only the real Discipline model (cut out with rembg human-seg + alpha matting, hair specks cleaned):
1. Dark studio (hustlewithkai style): near-black seamless background, soft top light, back view (photo 017), denim sinks into the dark, only logo + site + price.
2. Red type poster (Toji "DISCIPLINE" style): off-white paper, giant condensed distressed red DISCIPLINE, model in B/W in front (photo 000), "TALENT? ONLY" top-left, thin rule, plus marks; no character name, no motivational lines.
3. Smoky offer ad (muscleblaze style): charcoal smoke gradient, model 3/4 (photo 004) fading into the dark, small offer badge top-left (Rs. 899 / save Rs. 300 / site), Shop now pill.
Principles: no glitch/RGB split, no neon, generous negative space, small tracked type (DM Sans), real photo only; backgrounds/typography are graphics. Next step: animate these as minimal video ads.
